# Orbit Breaker Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 20, 2026

Orbit Breaker is designed to work without an account and without collecting personal information from the developer's servers.

## Data stored on your device

The game stores your best score, progression, cosmetic selections, accessibility settings, daily challenge score, and anonymous playtest aggregates locally on your device. This information is used only to operate and improve the game.

## Game Center

If you use Apple Game Center, Apple processes your player identity, leaderboard scores, and achievements under Apple's privacy terms. Orbit Breaker submits scores and achievement progress only after Game Center authentication. The developer does not receive your Apple ID credentials.

## Playtest data

Test builds record aggregate gameplay statistics locally, including completed runs, run length, restart count, score ranges, first-landing success, and failure reasons. The game does not automatically transmit these metrics. A tester may choose to export the report and provide it with feedback.

## Tracking, advertising, and third parties

Orbit Breaker contains no advertising SDK, cross-app tracking, analytics SDK, location collection, contact access, camera access, microphone access, photo access, or sale of personal information.

## Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on the device until the app is deleted or its data is cleared. Game Center data is controlled through Apple services and account settings.

## Children

Orbit Breaker does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

Updates to this policy will be published with a revised effective date.
