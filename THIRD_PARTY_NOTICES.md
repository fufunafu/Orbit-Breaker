## Kenney sound effects

Orbit Breaker uses selected effects from Kenney's Sci-Fi Sounds 1.0 and Interface Sounds 1.0 packs. Kenney created and distributed both packs under the Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal dedication.

- `launch.wav`: Sci-Fi Sounds `laserRetro_002.ogg`, converted to PCM WAV
- `land.wav`: Sci-Fi Sounds `impactMetal_002.ogg`, converted to PCM WAV
- `fail.wav`: Sci-Fi Sounds `explosionCrunch_002.ogg`, converted to PCM WAV
- `perfect.wav`: Interface Sounds `confirmation_002.ogg`, converted to PCM WAV
- `ui.wav`: Interface Sounds `select_002.ogg`, converted to PCM WAV

Source: <https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds> and <https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds>
