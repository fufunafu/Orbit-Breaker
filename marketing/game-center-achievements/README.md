# Game Center achievement artwork

These three localized English achievement images are ready for App Store Connect:

- `perfect-ten.png` for Perfect Ten
- `maximum-burn.png` for Maximum Burn
- `planet-runner.png` for Planet Runner

Each file is a 1024 by 1024 RGB PNG at 72 ppi without transparency. Important content remains centered for Apple's circular Game Center mask.

The `.gdignore` file keeps this App Store Connect-only artwork out of Godot's resource import pipeline. The iOS export preset also excludes the folder as a second packaging guard.

## Production prompts

The artwork was generated with the built-in OpenAI image tool on August 20, 2026, using the Orbit Breaker app icon and accepted achievement images as style references. The selected prompts were:

### Perfect Ten

Create a polished neon Game Center achievement icon for ten perfect landings. Center a cyan planet inside a ring of exactly ten magenta landing sparks, with one bright trajectory arc touching its center. Use a deep navy star field, vector-like gradients, and the Orbit Breaker cyan, white, and magenta palette. Keep critical content inside the central 75 percent. No text, numbers, logos, border, transparency, or watermark.

### Maximum Burn

Create a matching neon Game Center achievement icon for reaching a 5x combo. Center a sleek white and cyan spacecraft accelerating upward through exactly five energy rings that transition from cyan through magenta to gold. Keep critical content inside the central 75 percent. No text, numbers, logos, border, transparency, or watermark.

The initial Maximum Burn draft showed six rings. A targeted edit removed one central ring and rebalanced the remaining five while preserving the spacecraft, thrust, palette, lighting, and composition.

### Planet Runner

Create a matching neon Game Center achievement icon for landing on fifty planets. Show a bright cyan spacecraft following one continuous S-shaped orbital path through exactly seven distinct planets that recede upward and transition from cyan through violet, magenta, orange, and gold. Keep critical content inside the central 75 percent. No text, numbers, logos, border, transparency, or watermark.
