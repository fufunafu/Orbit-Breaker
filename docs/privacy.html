Privacy Policy

Effective August 20, 2026.

Orbit Breaker works without a developer account and does not send personal information or gameplay analytics to developer servers.

Local data

Scores, progression, cosmetic choices, settings, daily challenge results, and aggregate playtest metrics remain on your device unless you explicitly export them.

Game Center

If enabled, Apple processes player identity, leaderboard scores, and achievements under Apple’s privacy terms. Orbit Breaker never receives Apple ID credentials.

Tracking and device access

The game contains no ads, cross-app tracking, location access, contact access, camera access, microphone access, photo access, or sale of personal information.

Deletion

Delete the app to remove local data. Manage Game Center data through Apple services.

Contact support with privacy questions.
