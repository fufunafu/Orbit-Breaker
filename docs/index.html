How it plays

Three seconds to learn. A daily habit to master.

01 · ORBIT

Ride the circle

Your ship orbits the current planet on its own. Every lap lines you up with the next target, and every landing spins the orbit a little faster.

02 · WAIT

Watch the guide

A dashed line shows where you are pointed. One ring around the target means you will land. A double ring means the landing will be perfect.

03 · TAP

Break orbit

One tap launches you across the gap. Perfect landings chain into a combo multiplier up to 5x, and hazards end the run on contact.

What is waiting out there

An endless climb through three neon zones

Classic and Daily

Run endless Classic for your all-time best, or take on the Daily Challenge: the same deterministic layout for every player in the world, once per UTC day.

Perfect landings

Land inside the pulsing inner zone to score double and grow your combo. Perfect chains are the difference between a run and a record.

Asteroids and pulse mines

Hazards drift into your launch windows. Every layout is verified to keep a safe line open, so a clear shot always exists if you wait for it.

Ion Veil, Nova Drift, Sunforge

Climb far enough and the universe changes around you: new palettes, new music intensity, and a banner to welcome you in.

Skill-based loadout

Ships, trails, and planet themes unlock through play, never through purchases. Each locked item tells you exactly what it takes.

Game Center

All-time, weekly, and daily leaderboards plus achievements, with original adaptive music, haptics, and a saveable score card for every run.
